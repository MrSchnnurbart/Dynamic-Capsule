# Changelog — Dynamic Capsule

All notable changes to **Dynamic Capsule** are documented in this file.

## [1.0.0] - 2026-11-18

### Added / Nouveautés
- **Apple Dynamic Island Design**: Pure `#000000` OLED black capsule with organic 60/120 fps spring physics (`Like a Fruit` & `Like a Robot` presets) and zero idle CPU usage.
- **Universal Camera Cutout Safe Zone**: Precision Red Circle calibration guide (`Left`, `Center`, `Right`, or `Below Status Bar`) ensuring no icon or text is ever hidden behind the front camera lens on any Android phone.
- **0% Idle Opacity Mode**: Invisible at rest by default to prevent OLED burn-in while still detecting gestures over the camera area.
- **7 Reorderable Dynamic Cards**: Media Player, Active Calls, Timer & Stopwatch, Live Progress (Maps, Play Store, Telegram, Downloads), Flashlight, Volume Control, and Charging Status, with split-capsule support when 2+ activities run simultaneously.
- **17 System Mini-Capsule Events**: Device unlock, Charging start/stop, 5% Low battery warning, Battery Saver, Wi-Fi, Hotspot, VPN, Airplane mode, Location, Bluetooth audio, Sound mode, Do Not Disturb, Adaptive brightness, Day/Night theme, Auto-rotate, USB OTG, and AirPods/MaterialPods battery gauges.
- **5 Configurable Gestures & 16 Actions**: Single tap, Double tap, Long press, Swipe left, Swipe right, plus Swipe-up-to-close, assignable to Smart Scroll, Screenshot, App Search, Flashlight, Sliders/Shortcuts/Barcodes, Widgets, Direct Call, Custom Broadcast Intent, QR Code, Media Controls, Accessibility Actions, ChatGPT Assistant, Auto Brightness, Volume Mode, Lock Screen, and System Settings.
- **First-Launch Interactive Guide & Bilingual Support**: Full English and French (`EN` / `FR`) localization and step-by-step welcome tour.
- **GitHub Auto-Update System**: Built-in update checker and downloader powered by GitHub Releases (`MrSchnnurbart/Dynamic-Capsule`).
